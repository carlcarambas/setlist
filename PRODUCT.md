# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

Setlist is designed first for the **musicians and singers** of one church music ministry. They open a
shared plan on a phone, at practice or on stage, often with a weak signal or none, and read the sheets
from it. They don't have accounts.

Other people use the same app: whoever plans a service builds and orders its setlist and shares it, and
administrators sign in to keep the sheet library up to date. When their needs compete with a musician's
reading moment, the musician wins.

## Product Purpose

A music ministry keeps one PDF sheet library, builds a setlist for each service or event, and shares it
with the team. The moment the app has to get right is **playing from sheets**: find the next song and
read it, even with no internet. Offline is view-only; changes need a connection.

Success: without an account, a team member finds a song, opens the shared plan, keeps it offline, and
opens every sheet at church with no internet (`spec.md` §18).

## Positioning

**One current setlist.** Everyone sees the same, latest songs and order, instead of piecing it together
from a group chat and loose PDF files.

## Brand Personality

Calm, clear, dependable. It stays quiet and steady so nothing competes with the music. Wording is plain
and direct, and says what happened and what to do next.

## Anti-references

- A corporate dashboard: dense admin panels, charts, business-software chrome.
- A complicated tool: many menus, settings and steps.

## Design Principles

1. **The next sheet is never far.** Optimize the path from opening the app to reading a song above
   everything else.
2. **Everyone sees the same setlist.** Make it obvious what the current plan is, and when the device
   might be showing an older copy.
3. **Dependable offline.** Say plainly what works without a connection and what doesn't; never fail
   silently.
4. **Stay out of the way.** Fewer steps, controls and words; add something only when a musician or
   leader needs it.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Readable at a distance: large song titles, keys and sheets, usable on a music stand.
- Comfortable for older users: generous touch targets, plain wording, and layouts that hold up with
  larger text settings.
- From `spec.md`: keyboard accessible, respects reduced motion, and status is never shown by color alone.
