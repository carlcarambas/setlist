# Member trial (one real service)

**Who:** 3–5 ministry members, at least one using an iPhone and one using Android.
**When:** the week before a real service, through the service itself.
**Goal:** spec §18. Can members do the whole Sunday workflow without help?

## Send this to the members

> Hi! We're trying a new app for our setlists. No account needed.
>
> 1. Open **<live address>** on your phone and add it to your Home Screen (iPhone: Share → *Add to Home
>    Screen*; Android: *Install app*).
> 2. Find a song in **Library** and open its sheet.
> 3. Open the plan for Sunday (link below). Tap **Keep offline**.
> 4. On Sunday, use the app from your Home Screen at church, even with no signal.
> 5. Afterwards, tell me what was confusing or didn't work.
>
> Sunday's plan: <link from the Share button>

**Leader's tasks** (one person): create Sunday's plan, add and order the songs, add it to the event, and
send the link with **Share**.

## Success (spec §18)

Every member, without an account: found a song, viewed its sheet, used the shared plan, kept it offline,
and opened every sheet at church with no internet. The leader built, ordered and shared the plan.
Administrators can keep the library up to date.
